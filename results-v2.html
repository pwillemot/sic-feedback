Use-case priorities

The score, in one line
SCORE=V×E×(1 −backend)×popularity
  • V — vote-weighted business value (1–5)
  • E — vote-weighted effort today (1–5)
  • backend — integration penalty: low 0% · medium 10% · high 20%
  • popularity — more votes = higher score (the most-voted keeps 100%, the least-voted 70%)
Vote-weighted: each average is first nudged toward neutral (3) by the weight of ~3 votes, so a handful of votes can’t dominate; then popularity rewards the use cases the whole room rated.

The prize vs. the priority

Position = the raw prize: further up & to the right = more business value and more manual effort removed. The final priority also depends on back-end integration cost — shown by each bubble’s outline — so the label under every bubble is its rank & score from the list on the right. That’s why a top-right bubble can still rank low.
